# Exploratory Data Analysis of Computational Thinking Skills
*Academic Internship Project - University of the Basque Country (Bilbao)*

## 📌 Project Abstract 
This study presents a comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) investigating the Computational Thinking (CT) proficiencies and academic performances of a 181-student cohort at the Bilbao School of Engineering. The primary objective was to evaluate the efficacy of traditional mathematical assessments in predicting modern algorithmic aptitude and to uncover latent pedagogical disparities. By deploying statistical correlation modeling, isolated cohort comparisons, and unsupervised machine learning (K-Means clustering), the analysis yielded four critical insights. First, a significant “Pedagogy Gap” was identified, proving that an extreme lack of instructional standardization, rather than linguistic barriers (Euskera vs. Castellano), skews cognitive development. Second, an “Underdog Triumph” emerged: Environmental Engineering students significantly outperformed historically elite branches in core Algorithmic Thinking, exposing the structural flaws of traditional calculus-based exams. Third, clustering algorithms revealed a class-wide developmental bottleneck; students excel at problem decomposition but systematically fail at algorithmic synthesis. Finally, the data dismantled historical STEM stereotypes, as the female cohort demonstrated absolute cognitive dominance across all CT macro-skills. The report concludes by recommending a strategic pivot toward standardized computational materials, adaptive learning ecosystems, and project-based algorithmic synthesis. 

## Keywords: Computational Thinking, Engineering Education, Exploratory Data Analysis, Pedagogy Gap, Cognitive Profiling  

## 🔍 Key Findings
* **The "Underdog" Effect:** Discrepancies in algorithmic aptitude and final performance across different engineering disciplines (Environmental Engineering vs. Industrial Engineering).
* **The Pedagogy Gap:** Performance variations based on the language of instruction (Euskera vs. Castellano) and specific instructional cohorts.
* **Deconstructing STEM Stereotypes:** A comprehensive gender gap analysis revealing cognitive differences in macro CT skills.
* **Cognitive DNA & Profiling:** Unsupervised machine learning (K-Means & PCA) applied to categorize students into distinct cognitive profiles (e.g., Conceptual Leaders, Mainstream Practitioners, Critical Risk Group).
